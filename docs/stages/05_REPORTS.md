# Stage 05 — professional company reports

Prerequisite: validated observations and claim contracts. Current six-section pack is an extractive scaffold only.

Deliver roughly six detailed pages plus evidence appendix: executive assessment; business/offer; financial trends; operating KPIs/comparisons; risks/governance; management commentary and research questions. Generate executive summary last from approved section claims. Charts use observation IDs and tested calculations only. Every chart datum and material claim opens its original source. Missing data stays visible.

Report API must return coverage, claim IDs, chart specifications, conflicts and corpus/config snapshot. Export JSON plus print/PDF. Use document/PDF skill rendering and inspect pagination, dense tables, labels, units, citations and overflow. Never promise exactly six pages at the cost of omitted qualifications. Test short/long company names, no-data sections, negative series and mixed-period rejection.

Later transcript ingestion preserves speaker/role, date, prepared remarks versus Q&A and real paragraph/timestamp source anchors. Label guidance as forward-looking management statements.
